# Alexander coin catalogue anchor

The Fitzwilliam Museum records silver drachm **CM.G.17-R**, attributed to Alexander III, dated 336–323 BCE, with Amphipolis as its mint and a recorded weight of 4.07 g.

[Full museum record](https://data.fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/id/object/305013).

This is an ancient Macedonian coin reference, not a findspot claim about modern Skopje. Amphipolis is in present-day Greece. The catalogue page examined here does not supply enough visual detail to describe the design of this particular coin safely.

## Before adding an image

Match both sides to the exact accession number. Record whether a coin was struck during the ruler's lifetime or posthumously; a ruler's name alone cannot settle the date. Study field, figure, inscription and border as a small circular composition.
