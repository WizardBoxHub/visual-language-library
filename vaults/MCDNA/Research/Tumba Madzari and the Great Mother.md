# Tumba Madzari and the Great Mother

The Archaeological Museum describes a terracotta object from Tumba Madzari near Skopje, conventionally called the Great Mother or Magna Mater. Its 2020 account dates the displayed example to around 5000 BCE and associates it with a house and hearth context. A female upper body rises from a hollow rectangular house model, with openings in the sides.

The museum interprets the figure in relation to household protection and motherhood. Keep that interpretation labelled: the archaeological object survives, but its makers left no written explanation of its meaning.

[Object account by archaeologist Irena Kolištrkoska Nasteva](https://amm.org.mk/praistoriska-dama-za-museummonday/).

## Visual study prompts

Examine the joining of a human silhouette with architectural volume; compare the head, cylindrical torso and rectangular base. Study openings as shapes, rather than treating a reconstruction drawing as the original object.
