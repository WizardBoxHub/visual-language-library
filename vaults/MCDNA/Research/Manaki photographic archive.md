# Manaki photographic archive

The State Archive describes *Time Stands Still*, a project to digitise the photographic legacy of Milton and Janaki Manaki through the British Library's Endangered Archives Programme, supported by Arcadia.

[State Archive project account](https://arhiv.mk/en/time-stands-still/) · [EAP1470 project](https://eap.bl.uk/project/EAP1470)

The project page was identified, but access to item records was blocked by a browser-verification page during this research pass. Individual photographs have therefore not been catalogued or reproduced here.

## What to extract from an archival image

Record the archive reference, photographer, date or date range, place, caption language, depicted activity and rights statement. Separate the original caption from later interpretation. A posed portrait, a ceremony and a street scene each document different things.

Compare garment layering, tools, shop signs, interiors and gestures. Avoid treating every photographed costume as a timeless or universally worn national outfit. Related: [[Research status and next acquisitions]].
