# Research status and next acquisitions

## Current foundation

Four local photographs document Galichnik wedding dress and a contemporary ceremony. They have been visually checked, attributed and placed in a gallery and Canvas. Research notes cover a wider set of periods; those notes are not presented as image-complete boards.

## Access limits encountered

Wikimedia returned download limits, so requests were paused. The British Library archive required verification. A cultural-heritage PDF host refused the connection, and the folklore journal PDF was blocked. These texts and individual archive items have not been fully examined.

## Prioritised next acquisitions

- Regional embroidery: Dunje/Mariovo and Kumanovo, with whole-garment and detail pairs.
- Tumba Madzari: exact accession, object date and authorised full-object image.
- Trebenishte: catalogue entries and excavation context for masks and ornaments.
- Stobi and Heraclea: whole mosaic floors plus clearly located details.
- Kurbinovo, Nerezi and Ohrid: verified painting and icon records.
- Bigorski and Skopje carving: named workshops, dates and full interior context.
- Tetovo painted architecture and Ohrid domestic architecture.
- Manaki archive: item-level records for dress, craft and everyday life.
- Contemporary Macedonian artists and designers, with creator-led sources.

No blank or broken image cards have been inserted for these future acquisitions. No new website deployment has been made for this vault.
