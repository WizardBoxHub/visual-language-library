# Stobi mosaic research

The National Institution Stobi documents successive phases of the Episcopal Basilica and its baptistery. Deer, peacocks and a central vessel belong to the baptistery's Christian pictorial setting; their historical meaning should be studied with the building's function, not treated simply as generic animal decoration.

[Stobi, Episcopal Basilica](https://stobi.mk/en/stobi-2/the-city/the-episcopal-basilica/).

## Visual study prompts

Compare paired animals around a central axis, the vessel's silhouette, borders around the field, and the way tesserae turn around a contour. Use a whole-floor photograph to understand position before isolating a detail.
