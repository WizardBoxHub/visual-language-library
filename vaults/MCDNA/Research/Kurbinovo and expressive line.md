# Kurbinovo and expressive line

The state submission published on UNESCO's Tentative List dates the beginning of St George's painted decoration to 25 April 1191, based on an inscription. It emphasizes the dynamic drawing and elongated figures, especially Gabriel in the Annunciation.

[St George, Kurbinovo — Tentative List submission](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6492). This is a tentative-list entry, not an inscription on the World Heritage List.

## Visual study prompts

Follow the main gesture first, then trace how drapery reinforces or interrupts it. Compare long curves with sharply folded edges. Make a small silhouette study before a detailed drawing.
