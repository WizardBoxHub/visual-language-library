# Ancient worlds and their geography

The ancient section needs several connected strands: Neolithic settlements, Iron Age communities and exchange, Paeonia, ancient Macedon, Roman urban life, and late-antique Christianity. These labels describe different periods and political or archaeological settings.

Goran Sanev's research overview discusses Paeonian material evidence in the Vardar/Axios region, including bronze ornaments and elite burials. It also stresses uncertainties in identifying Paeonian origins and language. Imported objects in graves reveal exchange; they do not automatically identify the ethnicity of their owners.

Source: [Goran Sanev, Paeonia and the Paeonians](https://peoplingthepast.com/2025/09/26/blog-110-paeonia-and-the-paeonians-in-the-historical-and-archaeological-evidence-with-goran-sanev/).
