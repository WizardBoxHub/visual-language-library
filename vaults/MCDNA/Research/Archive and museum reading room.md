# Archive and museum reading room

## Sources consulted

- [Museum of Macedonia — Ethnology](https://museum.org.mk/page/etnologija): collection scope, dress and household craft.
- [Archaeological Museum — Tumba Madzari](https://amm.org.mk/praistoriska-dama-za-museummonday/): named object account.
- [Trebenishte exhibition account](https://amm.org.mk/izlozhbata-100-godini-trebenishte-otvorena-do-31-08/): catalogue and collection research route.
- [Goran Sanev — Paeonia](https://peoplingthepast.com/2025/09/26/blog-110-paeonia-and-the-paeonians-in-the-historical-and-archaeological-evidence-with-goran-sanev/): archaeological interpretation with bibliography.
- [Fitzwilliam — CM.G.17-R](https://data.fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/id/object/305013): individual ancient coin catalogue record.
- [Stobi — Episcopal Basilica](https://stobi.mk/en/stobi-2/the-city/the-episcopal-basilica/): archaeological setting for mosaics.
- [HAEMUS — Heraclea](https://haemus.org.mk/heraclea-lyncestis/): site account and bibliography.
- [UNESCO — Ohrid](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/99/) and [Kurbinovo submission](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6492): medieval heritage context.
- [UNESCO — Kopachkata](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/kopachkata-a-social-dance-from-the-village-of-dramche-pijanec-00995): living tradition and community context.
- [State Archive — Manaki project](https://arhiv.mk/en/time-stands-still/): photographic archive gateway.

## Further specialist routes

- [Marko Cepenkov Institute of Folklore](https://ifmc.ukim.mk/): journal *Makedonski folklor* and ethnological research. Relevant embroidery papers identified; full articles not yet read because access was blocked.
- [MANU dialect archive](https://dijalekti.manu.edu.mk/): a route for checking regional vocabulary and local names; not an image collection.
