# Ohrid and medieval painting

UNESCO identifies Ohrid as an important centre of medieval religious art and the dissemination of Slavic literacy and culture. Its heritage includes churches, frescoes, icons and later urban architecture. This gives the vault a substantial strand in painting and book culture, alongside archaeology and folklore.

[UNESCO, Natural and Cultural Heritage of the Ohrid region](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/99/).

## Visual study prompts

Study the direction of a gaze, the inclination of a head, the rhythm of drapery and the relationship of figure to architectural frame. For inscriptions, transcribe only legible text and retain the script and language attribution from a reliable catalogue.

Do not date an icon using the date of its photograph. Record the original church, present collection, painter where documented, and any later repainting.
