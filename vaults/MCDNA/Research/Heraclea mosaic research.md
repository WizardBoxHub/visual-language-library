# Heraclea mosaic research

Heraclea Lyncestis lies beside Bitola. HAEMUS documents the site's ancient road connections and its late-antique basilicas, with the Great Basilica's narthex mosaic an important focus for study.

[HAEMUS site account and bibliography](https://haemus.org.mk/heraclea-lyncestis/).

## Visual study prompts

Obtain a complete narthex view before selecting birds, animals, vegetation or borders. Record the precise building and floor location. Compare tessellated contours with the continuous lines of painting and the counted structure of embroidery without asserting a common origin.
