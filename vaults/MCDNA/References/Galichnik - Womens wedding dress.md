# Galichnik - Womens wedding dress

![[Attachments/178934837.jpg]]

The photographer identifies this museum-displayed ensemble as women’s wedding dress from Galichnik.

## Look closely

Study layered bands, the broad waist ornament and the stacked fringe against the pale undergarment.
