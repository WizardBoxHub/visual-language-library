Using SAVL

Browse

Open the visual atlas to scan images, or choose a theme from SAVL - Start here. Each image has a linked reference note. All downloaded photographs keep the source file’s proportions; some sources include borders or installation surroundings.

Search

Use Obsidian search for words such as spirals, stepped, silhouette, gold, or a maker’s name. Reference properties record maker, place, date, medium, themes, motifs and source. The favorite checkbox lets you mark references worth returning to.

Add a reference

Duplicate Templates/Reference template, save the full image in Attachments, and record the original page URL. Describe what you can see separately from cultural interpretation. Link at least one theme and two related references. Add the note to the relevant index or theme page; these pages are intentionally editable, not dependent on plugins.

Develop your own language

Study operations such as spacing, layering, framing and material contrast. Bring your own subject and purpose. Preserve the identities of living artists and communities; a personal experiment should be described as your interpretation. Source access does not by itself grant reproduction rights.

Expand next

The collection is selective; its existing references span ancient objects, Nasca geoglyphs, and modern and contemporary practices in several South American countries. Useful next additions include Southern Cone living textile practices, Guiana and Amazonian makers, Afro-South American practices, architecture and earthworks, each with specific attribution and full images.
