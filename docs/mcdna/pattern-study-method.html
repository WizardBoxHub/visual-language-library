MCDNA / Macedonian Visual Language

Pattern study method

Choose one documented reference and make six small studies:

  1. Silhouette: reduce the whole form to two values.
  2. Structure: identify its axis, bands, frames or repeated units.
  3. Rhythm: draw the spacing before the detail.
  4. Material: notice what stitch, tessera, carving or metal construction permits.
  5. Variation: change one formal feature while retaining the study's source.
  6. Context: write one sentence explaining what the original object was used for.

Label your new design “study after” or “inspired by” the reference. Do not present a newly invented motif as an inherited folk symbol.
