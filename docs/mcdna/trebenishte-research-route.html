Trebenishte research route

The archaeological exhibition 100 years of Trebenishte brought together expertise and collections from Macedonia, Serbia and Bulgaria. The museum reports a catalogue with contributions by 26 authors. This is a useful route into the excavation and collection history of the necropolis, rather than relying on isolated photographs of golden masks.

Archaeological Museum exhibition account.

Acquisition checklist

For each mask, ornament or vessel, obtain the grave number where available, excavation date, material, dating, current collection and object number. Compare a full object view with construction details. Keep the location of discovery separate from a proposed place of manufacture.

The catalogue has been identified, but its full text has not been examined in this pass. No local image is presented as a verified Trebenishte object yet. Related: Ancient worlds and their geography.
