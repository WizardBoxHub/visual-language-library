The museum collection has 53 references and 63 image files from The Met's public-domain image collection. It is a focused starting collection, not a survey of all Egyptian art. Every reference contains a local image and a museum source. Museum photographs are preserved without additional cropping. Selected detail views and modern facsimiles are labelled in their notes; a broken ancient object remains a fragment.

The separate hieroglyph library adds 100 symbol notes and 100 local PNG images, rendered from Noto’s font.
